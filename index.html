Privacy Policy for Matu Laa Bu

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Matu Laa Bu ("we", "us", or "our") handles user privacy for the Matu Laa Bu mobile application (the "App"). The App is developed by Van Lal Chhandam Takluem.

We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing a digital Christian hymn and song book experience.

2. Information Collection and Use

Matu Laa Bu is an offline digital hymn and song book application.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information through the App.

The App does not request or collect personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

The App does not require user registration or account creation.

3. Local Device Storage

The App stores certain non-personal information locally on your device to provide its features and customization options.

  • Song and hymn content: Hymns and song collections are stored locally on the device so they can be accessed without an internet connection.
  • Preferences and settings: Font selections, font colors, and custom gradient theme preferences are stored locally using Android DataStore.
  • Custom background image: If you choose to import a background image using the Android System Photo Picker, the selected image is processed and stored locally in the App's private storage on your device. The image is not uploaded to a server or shared with third parties by the App.

4. Third-Party Services and SDKs

Matu Laa Bu does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or external tracking services.

The App does not use Firebase, advertising SDKs, analytics services, or other external services to collect or transmit user information.

5. Permissions

Matu Laa Bu does not request dangerous runtime permissions.

Background image selection uses the standard Android System Photo Picker, allowing users to select an image without granting the App broad access to device storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Matu Laa Bu does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the App does not collect personal information, there is no personal information collected through the App for advertising, tracking, or user profiling purposes.

7. Data Security

Because Matu Laa Bu does not transmit personal information to our servers, the App does not maintain a remote database of user personal information.

Information stored locally by the App remains on the user's device unless the user removes the App or otherwise manages the device's stored application data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Matu Laa Bu, please contact:
